Using it on the clock

Placing the phone

Open the trunk door and stand the phone on the case floor, or rest it against the hood near the movement. Quiet room, fans off. Keep the screen on; the page asks the phone not to sleep while listening.

Putting it in beat

Aim for a ratio within 49.8 : 50.2 (beat error under 0.5% of the beat). Bend the crutch a very small amount at its loop, or slide an adjustable crutch, then let the pendulum settle and tap Reset. If the error grows, go the other way. Set beat with the case standing in its final position and fixed to the wall, since levelling the case shifts it.

Regulating

The rating nut under the bob sets rate: lower the bob to slow the clock, raise it to speed it up. On a seconds pendulum, 1 s/day is about 0.023 mm of bob travel. Use “Last 180 beats” for the fine reading. The phone's own clock is usually good to a couple of seconds a day, so check the final rating against a time signal over a week.

No live microphone here?

Record a minute or two of ticking with the phone's voice recorder, then tap Recording to analyse the file.
